# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Standards

**Expected:** clear, direct technical communication; accepting that you might be wrong;
disagreeing with a decision without disparaging whoever made it; helping newcomers understand
the reasoning rather than just the rule.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind; personal attacks; demeaning or exclusionary
comments; publishing others' private information; sustained disruption.

Technical bluntness is fine and often useful — "this reorders the fsync and will corrupt files"
is a good review comment. Contempt is not.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, the issue tracker, merge requests, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to `duongchausky@gmail.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will
respond, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban depending on severity — and will explain
the reasoning where it does not compromise the reporter.
